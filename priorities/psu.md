# Priorities — PSU

## What I actually want from this part

To not think about it, and to not have it take other parts with it when it dies. It should
have enough headroom that a future GPU doesn't force a second purchase.

## Ranked priorities

1. **Reliability.** This is the one part whose failure can cascade.
2. **Headroom for the 2028–29 GPU upgrade** without replacing the unit.
3. **Fully modular**, for build sanity and airflow.
4. **ATX 3.1**, for proper transient handling.
5. Efficiency rating. Real but overvalued.

## Realistic expectations

- **The dream build over-specified this by ~$135.** A 1000 W Titanium unit at $214.90 for a
  system that peaks around 550 W was buying headroom I'd never use at a premium I'd
  definitely pay. 750 W Gold at $75–80 is the right answer.
- **Efficiency matters slightly more here than on a normal desktop** — eleven hours a night
  under load, every night, is an unusual duty cycle. But "slightly more" does not justify
  $135 for a few percent. Gold is the correct stopping point.
- **The earlier $115 estimate was pessimistic.** Fully modular Gold ATX 3.1 units are
  available at $73–80.
- **Healthy market.** No shortage pressure, no urgency, buy on any ordinary sale.

## The absolute rule

**Never buy this part used.** The no-heavily-used-parts rule applies everywhere in this
build, but here it should be absolute rather than a preference. A degraded PSU fails in ways
that damage everything downstream, its condition is invisible from a listing, and the
savings are trivial. Buy it new, with its warranty, from a retailer.

Any Marketplace listing for a bare PSU should be ignored outright, regardless of price or
seller rating.

## What I'd trade away

Platinum/Titanium efficiency. Wattage above 750. Fancy cables. RGB. Brand prestige.

## What I would not trade

Fully modular, ATX 3.1, or dropping below 750 W. The savings below 750 W are trivial and
the headroom is the entire reason for buying at that number.

## Future goal

Carry the 2028–29 GPU upgrade on the same unit. If a future card genuinely needs more than
750 W, buying a second PSU in 2029 will still cost less than the Titanium premium would
today.
