# Priorities — GPU

## What I actually want from this part

Enough to drive the ultrawide at 120 FPS in the games where frame rate matters, and nothing
more. This is the part of the build I care least about, which is unusual and worth stating
plainly so I don't get talked into overspending on it.

## Ranked priorities

1. **16 GB VRAM.** At ~6 MP ultrawide this is the floor for a five-year card.
2. **Best value at 16 GB.** Vendor is open — Bazzite runs both. AMD currently wins on price
   and Linux driver quality, but that's a judgment to re-check, not a constraint.
3. **120 FPS** in Deadlock and Palworld; 90 is acceptable.
4. **Not heavily used**, with a real warranty if possible.
5. Absolute performance. Fifth.

## Realistic expectations

- **My most-played games don't need this part.** CK3, Stellaris and Cities Skylines II are
  simulation titles I deliberately cap at 60 FPS to keep game speed up. They're CPU and
  cache bound. The GPU is not what's limiting them and a bigger card won't fix them.
- **The market is inverted and I should not fight it.** New RDNA 3 cards are selling *above*
  their own used prices because they're end-of-life. Paying $799–895 for a new 7900 GRE or
  7800 XT would be paying a scarcity tax, not a price.
- **~$470 is the realistic number**, and a new RX 9060 XT at that price beats a used 7800 XT
  at $460 on warranty and wear alone.
- **The AI work does not use this card and probably won't.** It's CPU-based today.

## The CUDA question — resolved in my favour

Choosing Bazzite over SteamOS removed the AMD lock-in. Buying an AMD card now still means no
CUDA today, but **the 2028–29 upgrade can be NVIDIA** if the AI work ever moves to GPU
acceleration. I'm no longer committing the whole five years to one vendor, which was the part
of the SteamOS plan I liked least.

## What I'd trade away

Ray tracing. Peak frame rates. Anything above 16 GB. Brand loyalty.

## What I would not trade

Dropping below 16 GB VRAM to save money, or buying a heavily-used mining card because it
was cheap.

## Future goal

2028–29: upgrade into the PCIe 5.0 slot when the market is sane again and the games I play
have moved on. This card only needs to be good enough until then.
