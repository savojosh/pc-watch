# Priorities — Cooling

## What I actually want from this part

For heat to stop being the thing that limits my work. The machine this replaces is being
replaced *because* it thermally buckles. Cooling is a stated top-two priority alongside the
CPU, and it is not a place I want to economize.

## Ranked priorities

1. **Sustained load capacity.** Eleven hours a night, every night, all-core.
2. **Water.** A stated requirement — I want an AIO, not an air tower.
3. **Quiet enough to run overnight** in a room I might be sleeping near.
4. **Headroom for the Zen 6 chip** that goes in this same mount in 2027–28.
5. Looks. Distant fifth.

## Realistic expectations

- **This is a workstation thermal problem, not a gaming one.** The trading program runs
  23:00–10:00, caps at 80% of cores, and **throttles on CPU temperature** — so cooling
  capacity converts directly into completed work overnight. That's a much more demanding and
  more continuous profile than gaming, which is bursty.
- **I am knowingly taking a reliability loss.** A good air cooler has no pump and would
  likely outlast the build. An AIO has a wear part and a single point of failure. I should
  plan on a pump replacement or cooler swap inside the five-year window rather than treating
  it as a surprise.
- **A 240 mm AIO is not "great water cooling" for a 12-core chip under sustained load.**
  This matters for the Marketplace case bundle that includes one — that listing is a case
  purchase with a spare cooler, not a cooling solution.
- **The Arctic Liquid Freezer III was already right** on the old dream build and survives
  the platform change unchanged. Nothing about the AM5 switch alters this pick.
- **Open-box is genuinely low risk here.** A sealed AIO has nothing to wear in shipping.
  Newegg open-box has hit $52–61 against ~$95 new.

## What I'd trade away

RGB. Screen displays on the pump block. Brand. Fan noise at *peak* load — it can be loud
when I'm sitting at it, as long as it's quiet at the overnight duty cycle.

## What I would not trade

Water for air, or 360 mm for 240 mm. Both of those are the requirement itself, not a
preference around it.

## Future goal

Cool a Zen 6 X3D on the same mount without buying anything new. AM5 bracket compatibility
carries forward, so this cooler should be a one-time purchase for the life of the build —
minus the expected pump replacement.
