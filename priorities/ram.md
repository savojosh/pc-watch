# Priorities — RAM

## What I actually want from this part

Enough memory that I stop closing things. Right now the limit on how much I can have open
at once is memory, not patience — and the build only makes that worse by adding a Windows
VM on top of everything else.

## Ranked priorities

1. **Capacity.** 64 GB is the target. Everything else about RAM is secondary.
2. **Room to reach 128 GB** without discarding what I bought — hence 2×32, not 4×16.
3. **EXPO at DDR5-6000 CL30** so it trains without a fight.
4. Speed. Genuinely last. This workload is capacity-bound, not bandwidth-bound.

## Realistic expectations

- **This is the worst market in the build and it is not going to improve on my schedule.**
  DDR5 is up ~300% in six months on AI datacenter demand. A 64 GB kit that bottomed at $159
  is ~$869. Waiting for a good price is likely to mean waiting past this build.
- **32 GB is a floor I will hit weekly, not a compromise I'll forget about.** SteamOS host
  plus a Windows VM at 8–16 GB plus Dart analysis servers at 2–6 GB per project plus Gradle
  daemons plus emulators — the arithmetic runs out fast.
- **The 4-DIMM speed derate is real and I should not care.** Dropping to ~4800–5200 to gain
  32 GB is a good trade for VMs and compiles. It costs a little in gaming, where I'm not
  bandwidth-limited anyway.
- **Used DDR5 is worth watching for the first time ever.** That was never true before this
  year.

## What I'd trade away

Frequency above 6000. Tight secondary timings. RGB. Heatsink looks. All of it, for capacity.

## What I would not trade

Going below 32 GB at purchase, or buying 4×16 and capping myself at 64 GB forever. Both are
false economies that cost more later.

## The honest tension

RAM is roughly a third of this build's cost at current prices, and it is the least exciting
part of it. Every time the budget gets tight, RAM is the tempting cut — and it is the wrong
one, because the second kit will cost *more* later, not less. If something has to give,
it should be the second SSD, not the memory.

## Future goal

Get to 64 GB within six months of the initial build. That is the highest-value upgrade
available to this machine, ahead of both the GPU and the CPU. 128 GB only becomes relevant
if the CPU-inference ambition turns real.
