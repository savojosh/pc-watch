## Recommendation

**Watch Newegg open-box for the Arctic Liquid Freezer III 360.** It has hit **$52–61**
against ~$95 new — a 40%+ saving on what is genuinely one of the lowest-risk open-box
purchases in a PC build. A sealed AIO has nothing that degrades in shipping and no wear part
except the pump, which either works on first power-up or doesn't.

If no open-box appears, buy new at ~$91–95. No urgency; this price is stable.

## Filter rules for the nightly watch

**Ignore every air cooler, at any price.** Water is a stated hard requirement, not a
preference, and a great deal on an air tower is not a deal for this build.

Ignore 240 mm AIOs as primary coolers. Ignore AM4-only coolers without an AM5 bracket.
Ignore custom-loop components — out of scope.
