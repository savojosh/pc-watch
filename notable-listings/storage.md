# Notable listings — Storage

Spec: `../design-spec/storage.md` · Prices: `../market-prices/storage.md`
**Last verified: 2026-08-31**

## Recommendation

**Buy the 1 TB boot drive when you're ready to install, and defer the 2 TB.** Nothing in
this market is worth chasing right now — NAND is at 3–4× its floor and Black Friday will not
fix that. Buy the minimum needed to have a working machine and revisit in the new year.

The 2 TB drive is **the single cleanest budget cut in the build**. Partition the 1 TB as a
stopgap.

**Do not pursue used SSDs.** Remaining write endurance is invisible from a listing, the
drive holds everything you care about, and the savings in a shortage are smaller than they
look. This is a new-retail-only category.

## Action

1. Buy **1 TB Gen4 with DRAM** at ≤ $200 if it appears; otherwise buy at $239 when the build
   is ready to assemble.
2. **Alert at 2 TB Gen4 with DRAM < $250.** That's the threshold that un-defers drive two.
3. Re-check the tracker every two weeks; report any sustained two-week decline.
4. Before buying: measure the actual Steam library size. It's the biggest unknown driving
   drive-two capacity, and if it's large the answer is 4 TB rather than 2 TB.

## Filter rules for the nightly watch

Ignore: DRAM-less drives (including the Crucial P510 from the old dream build), QLC drives,
Gen5 drives, and all used SSDs — regardless of price.
