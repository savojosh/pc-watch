## Recommendation

**Try to negotiate the Corsair Crystal 680X down to $110–130**, or take the Lancool 205
bundle at $100 if the case is in the stated condition. Both are reasonable; neither is
urgent, and a new Lancool 216 at ~$95 is a perfectly good default that requires no
negotiating and no driving.

Cases are the safest used category in the build — no electronics, no wear parts, damage is
visible in photos — and they are **platform-agnostic**, so the AM5 switch retires none of
these listings.

## Checklist before buying any case

360 mm radiator clearance **in the position you'll actually use** · GPU clearance ≥ 330 mm ·
mesh or otherwise unrestrictive front · enough included fans · front-panel USB-C.

## Filter rules for the nightly watch

Ignore cases without confirmed 360 mm support, cases under ~$50, and anything marketed
primarily on 3.5" bay count — that requirement went away when the NAS became a separate box.
