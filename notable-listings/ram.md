## Recommendation

**Get the first 32 GB inside a Micro Center bundle.** At $404 for a standalone 32 GB kit,
the RAM is most of a bundle's value — it is by far the cheapest route to the first kit.

Beyond that: **buy early, not late.** This market has gone up every month this year. The
usual "wait for a sale" instinct is actively losing money here.

**Watch used DDR5 seriously.** At 5.5× the all-time low, secondhand memory is worth tracking
for the first time ever — a used 2 × 32 GB DDR5-6000 kit at ≤ $600 would be the single most
valuable find the nightly deal watch could produce.
