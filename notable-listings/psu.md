**None, and none should ever be pursued.**

## The absolute rule

**Ignore every bare PSU listing on Marketplace, at any price, from any seller.** The
no-heavily-used-parts rule applies across this build, but here it is absolute rather than a
preference:

- Degradation is completely invisible from a listing or a photograph
- Failure modes can damage every component downstream — this is the one part that takes
  others with it
- The savings are trivial: $30 on a $75 part
- The warranty is most of what you're actually buying

A PSU included inside a whole-system purchase is a different question and gets judged on the
system's merits — see `whole-systems.md`.

## Action

1. Buy either candidate at ≤ $80 whenever convenient. No timing skill required.
2. Consider an **850 W Gold at ≤ $90** — a little extra headroom for the 2028–29 GPU upgrade
   for very little money.
3. Do not pay above $100 for 750 W.

## Filter rules for the nightly watch

Ignore: all used and open-box PSUs · Bronze-rated units · non-modular units · SFX units ·
anything below 750 W. Report only new, fully modular, 750 W+ Gold ATX 3.1 units under $80.
