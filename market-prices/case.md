## Reading the market

There is no pressure here and no reason to stretch. $85–100 buys a genuinely good airflow
ATX case with 360 mm support. Prices are stable year-round with regular ordinary sales.

**The dream build's North XL at $156** was a full tower chosen partly for drive capacity —
which no longer matters now that the NAS is a separate box. At $60 more than a Lancool 216
for no functional gain on this build, it is no longer the right pick.

## Used market assessment

Cases are **platform-agnostic** — the AM5 switch does not retire any case listing. They are
also one of the safer used buys: no electronics, no wear parts, damage is visible in photos.

- **Lancool 205 Mesh + CM Elite 240 + fans, $100** — roughly break-even against new for the
  case alone, with a spare 240 mm AIO thrown in. Reasonable. **Value the AIO at $0** for
  decision purposes; it does not meet the cooling spec.
- **Corsair Crystal 680X + 3× LL140, $160 OBO** — excellent dual-chamber radiator support,
  but glass-fronted, which cuts against the airflow priority. Fair, not a steal. **Target
  $110–130.**

## Buy triggers

- **Any target case new at ≤ $95**: buy.
- **Any target case new at ≤ $80**: strong buy.
- **Used airflow ATX with confirmed 360 mm support at ≤ $60**, good condition, rated seller:
  flag it.
- Do not pay above $110 for a case in this build.

## Do not track

Cases without confirmed 360 mm radiator clearance. Cases under ~$50 (the fan and build
quality cost more in frustration than they save). Cases bought primarily for 3.5" drive
bays — that requirement was removed when the NAS became a separate box.
