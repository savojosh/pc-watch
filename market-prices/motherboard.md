## Reading the spread

The class runs **$170–230**. The $60 premium over the ASRock Steel Legend buys exactly one
thing: an expansion slot that stays usable when all four M.2 slots are filled. That is worth
paying for here because the Realtek NIC risk makes a spare slot non-theoretical.

**X870 is currently cheaper than B850** in this sample ($182 vs $230), which is unusual and
worth knowing — but the X870 Pro RS has *fewer* usable M.2 slots and kills its x4 slot when
M2_2 is populated. Cheaper, and worse, for this build.

## Buy triggers

- **Take it at ≤ $230.** This is already the normal price; don't wait for a sale that may
  not come.
- **Strong buy under $200.**
- **Inside a Micro Center 3-in-1 bundle**, the board's effective price is far lower — this
  is the preferred purchase route. Check `cpu.md` before buying the board standalone.

## Cautions

- Order the **MAX WiFi**, not the **MAX WiFi II**. $30 difference, near-identical names.
- Non-WiFi variants of some boards ship an empty M.2 Key-E slot with no module — check the
  SKU includes the WiFi card.
- Board prices creep upward as a chipset ages and stock thins. This is not a part to wait
  a year on.

## Do not track

AM4, LGA1700 or LGA1851 boards at any price. Boards with fewer than 4 DIMM slots. Boards
whose only secondary slots are x1 electrical.
