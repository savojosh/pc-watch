## The open-box opportunity

This is the one part in the build where **open-box carries genuinely little risk.** A sealed
AIO has no consumables that degrade in shipping and no wear parts beyond the pump itself,
which is either working or not on first power-up. Newegg open-box has hit **$52–61** against
~$95 new — a 40%+ saving on a low-risk purchase.

Worth actively watching for, unlike open-box storage or PSUs.

## Buy triggers

## Do not track

**Any air cooler, at any price.** Water cooling is a stated hard requirement and an air
tower does not satisfy it regardless of how good the deal is. This is a filter rule for the
nightly deal watch, not a preference.

**240 mm AIOs as a primary cooler.** Undersized for a 12-core chip under an eleven-hour
sustained overnight load. A 240 mm unit bundled with a case is a bonus, not a solution — see
the Lancool 205 listing in `../notable-listings/case.md`.

AM4-only coolers without an AM5 bracket. Custom loop components — out of scope for this build.

## Also note

Do not buy separate thermal paste. The Liquid Freezer III ships with paste pre-applied; the
dream build's $9.99 MX-6 line was free money.
